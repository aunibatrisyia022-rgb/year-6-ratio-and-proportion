♡ ABOUT RATIO ♡

Think of a ratio as a way of comparing two groups. Look at the objects first, then write the numbers in the correct order.

📌

What is a ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities. We can write it using a colon (:).

  • 4 cats : 6 dogs
  • 2 red : 3 blue
  • 5 apples : 2 oranges
🍓

Remember this!

The order matters. If we have 3 strawberries and 2 blueberries:

3 : 2

It is NOT the same as 2 : 3.
